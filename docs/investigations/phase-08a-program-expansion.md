Research date: **2026-11-09 (America/New_York)**. Target: **Rutgers–New Brunswick undergraduate, 2026–2027 catalog**. Base: `origin/main` at `0c835c8`. This is an inventory/design report, not a reviewed encoding or authorization to implement Phase B.

## Decision summary

- Discovery found **516 navigation leaves**, including **172 subject-coded pages**. Neither number is a major count. Live navigation matched the archived tree, with zero added/removed URLs. **135 public catalog pages** were fetched successfully, including two uncoded Bloustein pages added during reconciliation.

Rows include degree variants, a renamed program, a sunsetting program, and separately documented school routes. **This is not 70 distinct active majors.**
- Reuse the requirement tree, inherited grade floors/quotas, variants, school-set composition, eligibility reconciliation and term-scoped SOC certifications. Actual gaps concern applicability/cohorts, admission evidence, cross-school shared majors, richer school/attempt policies, and the versioned membership index.
- Recommended pilots: **SAS CS BS**, **RBS BAIT BS**, **SoE Industrial Engineering BS**, **SEBS Microbiology BS**, plus **Biological Sciences BA under both SAS and SEBS**. CS BS already has a validated local encoding.
- Human resolution is needed for conflicting RBS cohort/declaration wording, Business Ethics course numbers, Biology credit totals, EnvE credit totals, and cohort-specific school rules before publication.

### Inventory boundary and reading key

STEM here includes the requested natural/life sciences, mathematics/statistics, engineering and computing/technical curricula, including Cognitive Science and ITI. The catalog supplies no single authoritative STEM flag. Quantitative, environmental, health and spatial-design boundary programs are visible separately; **this is not a federal CIP/STEM-OPT classification**. All RBS majors and all catalog-listed SoE curricula are included regardless of that boundary. SEBS pages were swept broadly to catch cross-school and applied programs.

SAS/SEBS Biology and Exercise Science have explicit cross-school references. Permission to add another school's second major does not make that program a SEBS first-major route. Minors, optional certificates/concentrations, first-year engineering code 004, transfer-preparation code 001, preprofessional advising and graduate endpoints are not independent new majors. Astrobiology, Astronomy, Archaeology, Architectural Studies and Health and Society are minor-only in the examined sources. Sport Management's placement in the Life Sciences division does not make it a biology major.

The catalog's SC&I “Curriculum Overview” contains **graduate MCM** requirements: exclude it from undergraduate ITI. Pharmacy's professional PharmD and SAS nursing/referral pages require a separate professional/health scope decision; no standalone NB nursing major was found in this catalog navigation. Do not invent BA/BS majors from those referrals.

`P` = prose, `L` = course lists/headings, `T` = HTML tables. A semester-formatted curriculum does **not** establish mandatory semester timing. Explicit prescribed curricula may establish course membership; sample four-year plans remain advice. “No separate variant identified” describes these sources, not proof that no department-approved option exists elsewhere.

## Course knowledge index plan — design only

### Contract and reused authority

The index answers **which versioned program requirements a course might fill in a given term**. It does not decide that a student needs it, may enroll, meets grades/residency/admission, or can double-count it. Search, Options, planner and assistant should consume one deterministic membership service; the assistant receives its tools' results.

Reuse reconciled `requirement_course_option` for curated major/minor lists and predicates; `offering_core_code` for the exact term's certifications; explicit program/school/component applicability and lifecycle. Retain original categories, sequence groups and rule provenance. A flat bag of course codes cannot replace an OR-of-sequences or overlap constraints.

### Proposed logical projections

These are schema proposals, not migrations:

| Projection | Fields / invariant |
|---|---|
| `knowledge_build` | Build id, catalog scope, component/definition hash manifest, review/lifecycle revision, offering/tag revision, source snapshots, builder/rule version, start/completion time, ready/stale/failed state and coverage counts. |
| `knowledge_membership` | Build, course, program-context/version, component/version, requirement node, system, term, category/sequence group, rule/source/hash, support/review label, conditional-evidence descriptors. Uniqueness preserves meaningful category/provenance distinctions. |
| `knowledge_context_coverage` | Build/context/term, major/minor/school/Core completeness, unknowns, expected/observed counts, certification availability, degree-credit/free-elective-policy coverage. Completeness is explicitly established, never inferred from zero rows. |
| Published pointer | Atomically select an immutable validated ready build per scope/revision; never clear the live index while rebuilding. |

Shared component membership can be stored once and joined through program-context applicability, avoiding duplicate authored trees and a dense physical course × major × school cross-product. Materialize the contextual read view only if measurements justify it. Minors need their own sourced/reviewed rules; navigation names alone are insufficient.

### Coverage, classification and “free elective only”

Enumerate **every undergraduate course offering in every loaded term**, not the planner shortlist or keyword hits. Maintain requirement-side evidence for named courses with no loaded offering: “not offered here” differs from “not a requirement.” Graduate courses enter only through an explicit reviewed exception.

- **Mapped:** source-backed structural major/minor/school/Core memberships, carrying constraints and review labels.
- **Free elective only:** permitted only when reviewed context coverage and term-certification coverage are complete, no structured requirement applies, and school/program degree-credit policy permits the course. This is structural eligibility, not the student's remaining requirement status.
- **Excluded:** a sourced program/degree-credit restriction prevents counting; retain its scope and reason rather than hide it.
- **Unknown/unreviewed:** missing encodings or departmental lists, incomplete certifications, adviser-only decisions, ambiguous cohorts, stale sources or unreviewed programs. **A missing membership row must never mean “free elective only.”**

If a mapped requirement is already met, the course remains structurally mapped; query-time allocation decides additional usefulness. A P/NC attempt may earn only free credits while a graded attempt in that same course can satisfy a requirement. Do not put grades, attempts, GPA, student ids or remaining-status results into the shared index.

### Term, source and constraint rules

Use the requested term's tags, including SOEHS, ECN and GVT; do not silently union certifications across years. An unpublished/missing term needs unknown status or an explicit historical estimate. Curated lists are structurally versioned; offering availability/enrollment validity remain separate gates.

Join with home school, catalog year, admission cohort, variant, node/category and source/hash. Retain alternative/sequence/overlap structure and exclusion scope. Title similarity or BM25 relevance must not grant degree membership, a grade floor, admission or experiential approval. Review changes must update labels even when membership pairs stay identical; source withdrawal and removals must invalidate the build.

### Rebuild, rollout and 16 GB memory budget

1. Mark affected contexts/terms dirty transactionally when definitions/components, eligibility pairs, sources/reviews, course identities/equivalencies, school/cohort crosswalks or offering tags change. Use a durable revision/outbox across ingestion/backend processes; an in-memory callback is insufficient.
2. Build an immutable revision in bounded context/term batches, reusing shared components. Do not allocate every course × every program × every term in memory. Measure row counts, query plans and peak RSS before choosing joined versus materialized layers. No embeddings or model call is required.
3. Validate offering coverage, node references, provenance, categories, exclusion handling, lifecycle labels and deltas from the prior revision. Failed builds stay unpublished. A last-known-good result may be served only with an explicit stale label where policy allows; never answer absence questions as current from stale data.
4. Shadow current reconciler/read paths and compare memberships. Switch Search/Options, planner and assistant tools to one service behind a controlled rollout, retaining deterministic validity/allocation gates. Remove duplicate inference only after parity.
5. Identical input manifests produce identical content hashes/pairs. A review revision can change support labels without changing those pairs.

### Future acceptance checks — not executed in Phase A

- A newly ingested course enters every applicable context even if never shortlisted; removal of a rule/tag removes membership in the new revision.
- Membership can differ by term, variant and home school while shared major content stays canonical.
- Categories, whole sequences, overlap caps and exclusions survive projection; no admission/grade/residency decision is inferred.
- Missing elective lists, unreviewed majors and sparse experiential tags produce unknown, never a false free-elective claim.
- Query-time student overlays remain owner-scoped; no student data enters shared projections.
- Failed/repeated/concurrent builds cannot expose a partial or falsely current snapshot.
- Any future database tests use only `*_test`; frontend/backend suites run sequentially and artifacts stay outside OneDrive.
